## Check your setup

After installing, connect your Val Town account and start a new chat or session. Ask: “Use Val Town to list my vals. Don’t create or change anything.” A response from Val Town, even an empty list, confirms the connection. See [setup troubleshooting](/guides/prompting/plugin#troubleshooting) if it doesn’t work.

## Tips, Troubleshooting

- Ask to list or examine your vals or read sqlite data, logs, etc.
- Ask your LLM to edit vals and even agentically iterate
- Broken code? Visit the `Versions` tab or `History` of your val to revert
- Large changes? Use feature [branches](/vals/branches) and [merge](/vals/pull-requests) when stable
- Need help? Ask in the Val Town [Discord server](https://discord.val.town/)
